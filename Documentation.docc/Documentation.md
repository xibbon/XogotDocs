Build, run, and ship games with an Apple-native editor that reads and writes standard Godot projects.

## Overview

Xogot is a native port of the Godot editor for Apple platforms. Projects, scenes, and scripts are all interchangeable with desktop Godot. Start a game on iPad and continue on your desktop, make changes on the go on your iPhone, and collaborate with teammates using Godot on Windows, Mac, or Linux.
