# Testing Xogot for Mac Preview

Xogot for Mac brings the Xogot experience to macOS: a native Mac app 
for creating, editing, testing, and shipping Godot projects, with a 
focus on Apple platform workflows.

Download Xogot for Mac and learn more about the open beta on the 
 [Xogot for Mac](https://xogot.com/mac) page.

For a guided introduction to the preview, start with the
<doc:Xogot-for-Mac-Tutorials>. The <doc:00.xogot_for_mac_tour> gives you
a quick walkthrough of the Mac-native workspace, project navigation,
Command Palette, scene selection, and run destination menu. It is also
the best starting point if you want to get oriented quickly and continue
on to signing and deploying your projects on mobile devices.

Thank you for your interest in testing Xogot for Mac. This page explains 
what we are looking for in the preview and how to provide feedback.

Xogot layers a native Mac experience on top of the Godot editor engine, 
so projects should remain compatible with the desktop edition of Godot. 
Our goal is to make it easy to move projects between Godot and Xogot on 
iPad, iPhone, and Mac while improving the experience for developers 
building on and targeting Apple platforms.

One of our principles is to preserve the capabilities needed for project 
compatibility and interoperability. If something you need is missing or 
does not behave as expected, we generally want to hear about it. At the 
same time, Xogot for Mac gives us an opportunity to simplify and improve 
parts of the Godot workflow, especially around Mac, iPhone, iPad, and 
Apple platform deployment.

## Providing Feedback

We really want to hear your feedback. You can give us feedback in any 
of these ways:

- Send us an email at support@xogot.com
- File an issue on [github.com/xibbon/XogotIssues](https://github.com/xibbon/XogotIssues)
- Join the #mac channel on the [Xogot Discord server](https://discord.gg/TDEcyfHZAh)

## What to Test

### Bugs in Xogot for Mac

We are interested in finding bugs, crashes, and unexpected behavior 
in the application. If you find a bug, please describe what you were 
doing when it happened and, if possible, include steps to reproduce it.

### Project Compatibility

Xogot for Mac is intended to maintain compatibility with Godot projects. 
Please let us know if a project behaves differently in Xogot than it does 
in Godot, especially if the issue affects editing, running, saving, or 
moving the project between tools.

### Mac User Experience

We want Xogot to feel like it belongs on macOS. Please tell us about 
anything that feels confusing, awkward, unfinished, or not Mac-like, 
especially in high-traffic parts of the editor.

### Apple Platform Debugging and Deployement

One of the goals of Xogot for Mac is to make Apple platform development 
more approachable for Godot developers. We are especially interested in 
feedback related to running, testing, debugging, packaging, and deploying 
projects for Mac, iPhone, and iPad.  

Check out our <doc:Xogot-for-Mac> for more details on signing and 
deploying your projects to iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

### Missed Opportunities

We have prioritized the areas we believe are most important for the 
initial preview, but we may have missed workflows that matter to you. 
If there are parts of the editor or Apple platform workflow where Xogot 
could provide a better native experience, please let us know.
